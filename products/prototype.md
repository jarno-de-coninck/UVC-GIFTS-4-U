# Prototype

A clickable prototype of the GIFTS-4-U Webshop and Control Tower, built from the wireframes and wireflows and styled with the GIFTS-4-U styleguide.

**File:** 'products\prototype\high-fidelity-prototype.html'

## Open it
Open `prototype/high-fidelity-prototype` in a browser. Use the switch at the top to move between Webshop and Control Tower.

## Included
- **Webshop:** homepage, products, product details, cart, checkout (with payment success or failure), order lookup, order tracking, return request, login, register, account
- **Control Tower:** dashboard with filters and stock, order details with flag and partner actions, insights, reorder thresholds

## Try
1. Webshop: add a product to the cart, go to checkout, choose "Test: payment fails", then try again with another payment method.
2. Control Tower: open the "Exceptions" tab, open order GFT-10482 and flag it.
